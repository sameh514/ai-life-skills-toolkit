# Evidence basis for workbook decisions

This is a compact design ledger, not a claim that the complete workbook package
has been tested as one intervention.

| Decision | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Effortful retrieval plus feedback | Retrieval-practice meta-analyses and classroom synthesis; feedback meta-analysis | Strong for retention, moderate for far transfer |
| Match retrieval to explain/trace/debug/code | Pan & Rickard (2018) transfer meta-analysis | Response similarity matters; far transfer remains limited |
| Visible example -> faded completion -> independent problem | Atkinson, Renkl, & Merrill (2003); expertise-reversal literature | Moderate cross-domain evidence; exact CS fade is inferred |
| Self-explain causal steps | Bisra et al. (2018) meta-analysis | Moderate; explanations need checking |
| Block initial procedure, then interleave confusable types | Brunmair & Richter (2019) meta-analysis | Task-dependent, not universal |
| Confidence before feedback | Butler, Karpicke, & Roediger (2008); Guo (2022), doi:10.1111/jcal.12650, on metacognitive prompts in computer-based environments | Useful for calibration; Guo is an indirect fit rather than direct evidence for this exact confidence prompt |
| Genuine spacing across sessions | Cepeda et al. (2006, 2008); Mawson & Kang (2025); Latimier et al. (2021) | Spacing is reliable; expanding gaps show no reliable advantage over uniform gaps, so use configurable adaptive dates rather than privileging one pattern |
| Parsons/ordering only as a bridge | Du et al. (2020); Ericson et al. (2022); Hou et al. (2022) | Promising scaffold, insufficient as code-writing proof |
| Optional focus/access tweaks when requested | NICE NG87 environmental modifications | Guideline-backed modification class; use minimally and keep exact timing personal |

Important cautions:

- Karpicke & Blunt's 2011 retrieval-versus-concept-mapping comparison had a
  memorization/time confound. One preregistered re-examination (Mayrhofer et al.
  2023, `N=230`, one-week delay) removed the retrieval advantage when the
  mapping group also memorized; its mapping advantage on inference rested on
  two questions. Treat this as one study, not a settled correction.
- Rawson & Dunlosky's “three plus three” schedule was study-specific.
- Ariga & Lleras did not establish a universal Pomodoro interval; adjacent work
  by Helton and Russell found rest breaks could outperform task-switch breaks.
- Audio during moderate exercise has limited direct evidence for spoken recall,
  not for code, diagrams, or debugging.
